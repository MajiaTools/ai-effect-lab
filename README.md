## What it does

- **Text-to-video or image-to-video** from a library of editable effect prompts (zombie couple, flying car stunt, Burning Bridges party, Bulin 47 freestyle, ADÉLA dance, flood effect, orbit, explosion and more)
- Each effect page **explains the input, the workflow, and what to check** in your result
- **Two models in one workspace** — Seedance 2.5 and MiniMax H3; available inputs, durations and resolutions depend on the selected model
- Browsing effects and editing prompts is free; **the credit cost is shown before you submit**, and confirmed failures return credits

## How to use

1. Open https://aieffectlab.com and pick an effect (or describe one clear shot: subject, action, setting, camera movement)
2. Choose inputs and settings — reference media, model, duration and resolution — then review the displayed credit cost
3. Generate, then inspect movement, faces and continuity; find completed videos in My creations
